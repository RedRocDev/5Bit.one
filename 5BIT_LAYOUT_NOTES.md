# 5bit distinct-layout concept

This copy starts from `5bit-clean-baseline` and deliberately changes the visual grammar of the site rather than only changing words and colours.

## Structural changes

- Floating rounded navigation dock instead of a full-width glass header.
- Left-aligned asymmetric hero with screenshots embedded into the hero rather than a centred hero followed by a carousel.
- Platform support rendered as a compact rail.
- Product explanation rendered as an editorial numbered sequence instead of repeated feature cards.
- Screenshot section converted into a bento wall using all six 5bit screenshots.
- Theme Designer promoted to a major 5bit identity section with the silly preset names.
- Integrations reduced to a compact pill rail instead of a five-card clone-style grid.
- Final CTA uses a status/editorial treatment instead of a generic donation/download card.
- Background changed from the old grid language to a dot field with large asymmetrical glow fields.
- Active navigation changed from an underline to a filled gradient tab.

## Still intentionally unchanged

Guide, Downloads, Integrations, and Theme Designer content come from the cleaned baseline. They can receive their own separate layout redesign later after this homepage direction is approved.
